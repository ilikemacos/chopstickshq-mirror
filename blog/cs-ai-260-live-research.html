cs.AI 2.6.0 — Live research as of today

cs.AI 2.6.0 treats the calendar as a first-class input. Every question runs Chromium web research dated to today, so answers are not stuck on last year’s cutoff.

What’s new

  • Dated search on every prompt — queries are researched as of today unless you turn search off
  • Launch briefing — once per UTC day the Mac app fetches current date and news context
  • Welcome sheet — still appears after each version update

Fast, Standard, Pro, and Code are unchanged. Signed-in Pro still uses Groq GPT-OSS 120B on our server key. Free allowance still resets every 5 hours.
