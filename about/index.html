About Chopsticks HQ
Small lab. Sharp tools.

Chopsticks HQ is an independent software lab for free, local-first tools and lightweight games. We ship under one domain so installs, docs, and releases stay easy to find — without accounts, subscriptions, or product telemetry.

Why the HQ exists

Most “system tools” either lock features behind a paywall or bury installs in store pages and accounts. We wanted a quieter path: open downloads, readable installers, and apps that treat the Mac as the source of truth — not a login server.

chopstickshq.com is the master domain. Each product gets a stable path (for example /rnitro/ and /fathom/) and optional product subdomains when DNS is set. GitHub holds source and release mirrors under ilikemacos.

Principles

How we work

Local-first

Sensors, battery math, and menus run on your machine. Optional network features (weather, AI chat, update checks) are explicit and limited.

No product accounts

You do not create a Chopsticks login to use rNitro or Fathom. Keys for third-party AI stay in Keychain on your Mac.

Readable shipping

App ZIPs, PKG/DMG when useful, and shell installers you can open before running. Channels (Stable / Beta / Experimental) are labeled on the product pages.

Free core

Menu bar monitoring and free-tier battery tools stay free. “Pro” unlocks are optional experiments — not a SaaS meter for basic stats.

Honest marketing

We do not claim affiliation with Apple, iStat Menus, Stats, or game studios. Third-party marks belong to their owners.

AS IS software

Everything is provided without warranty. Back up important data before installing. See the full software disclaimer.

Channels & quality bar

Stable / Final is meant for daily use. Beta adds features that still need feedback. Experimental can change or break without ceremony — use when you want the newest toys.

Gatekeeper may require right-click → Open on first launch for ad-hoc signed builds. Prefer official paths under chopstickshq.com or the matching GitHub release assets.

Privacy in one paragraph

Product installs do not create Chopsticks accounts. Core monitor and battery apps do not phone home usage analytics. Update checks may read a public version.json on this site. AI features only contact the provider you configure. Vault keys are generated in the browser for unlock tokens — they are not free OpenAI or OpenRouter cloud keys.
