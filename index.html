About the HQ

Who we are, what we ship, and how we treat your machine.

Chopsticks HQ is a small independent lab for free, local-first software — macOS tools, browser games, and experiment pages. We publish under chopstickshq.com so every product has a clear home path, honest installers, and docs you can read without an account wall.

01

Master domain

chopstickshq.com is the hub. Product sites live under /rnitro, /fathom, /fathom-pro, /doomlike-fps, /minecraft, and more.

02

Own the stack

Native apps where it matters (Swift on Mac), simple ZIP / curl installers, and static product pages — no heavy framework tax on the download site.

03

Privacy-first

No product accounts. No telemetry from rNitro, Fathom Air, or Fathom Pro. Optional AI uses your provider keys and talks only to that provider.

04

Free by default

Core tools ship free. Premium experiments (like Fathom Pro unlock keys) stay client-side and optional — never a cloud subscription for basic sensors.

05

Ship in public

Releases land on this site and GitHub · ilikemacos with versioned ZIPs, changelogs, and shell installers you can inspect.

06

Honest scope

We build small sharp tools — not a full productivity suite. If something is Beta or Experimental, the page says so.

What we build

The HQ is the catalog for everything under the chopsticks brand. Tools focus on system visibility and battery honesty on Apple Silicon. Games and portfolio pages stay lightweight and self-hosted so they load fast and work offline once cached.

  • rNitro — free menu bar system monitor (CPU, temp, battery, GPU, RAM, network).
  • Fathom Air — free local-only battery drain app for macOS.
  • Fathom Pro — Air core plus weather and multi-provider AI (your keys).
  • Doomlike-FPS™ — browser FPS experiment.
  • Minecraft portfolio — server work and live status for imik2261_.
  • Guides & vault — how-tos, install notes, and the Pro unlock vault.

How we treat your Mac

Sensor apps read system APIs (IOPS, IOKit, pmset, IOReport) on-device. Installers prefer readable shell scripts or App ZIPs you unzip yourself. We do not sell “cloud dashboard” accounts for these tools. Full legal text lives on the software disclaimer and product terms pages (for example rNitro Terms).
